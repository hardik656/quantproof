Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 26, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Trading Data: When you use QuantProof, we collect trading data you voluntarily provide, including:

  • CSV files containing trade history, prices, volumes, and timestamps
  • Live broker data via API connections (with your explicit consent)
  • Strategy parameters and validation settings

Technical Data: We automatically collect certain technical information:

  • IP address and browser information for security
  • Usage patterns and feature interactions
  • Performance metrics and error logs

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide our service: Analyze your trading strategies with our 44 institutional checks
  • Generate reports: Create Fundable Scores and detailed analysis
  • Improve our algorithms: Enhance validation accuracy and feature development
  • Security: Detect fraud, abuse, and technical issues
  • Support: Respond to your questions and provide customer service

3. Data Security and Protection

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit (SSL/TLS) and at rest
  • Access controls: Only authorized personnel can access your data
  • Data retention: Trading data is retained for 30 days unless you purchase a PDF report
  • Broker security: Live broker connections use OAuth 2.0 and API tokens

Important: We never sell your trading strategies or personal information to third parties.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We share data only in limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Cloud hosting, analytics, and security services
  • Legal requirements: When required by law or court order
  • Business transfers: In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

Broker integrations: When you connect a broker account, we only access the specific trade data you authorize. We never store your broker credentials.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to certain data processing activities

To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Essential cookies: Enable basic site functionality
  • Analytics cookies: Understand how our service is used
  • Security cookies: Protect against fraud and abuse

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect site functionality.

7. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed and stored in India and other countries where our service providers operate. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international data transfers.

8. Children's Privacy

QuantProof is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete it immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by:

  • Emailing the address associated with your account
  • Posting a notice on our website
  • Displaying a prominent notice in our service

Your continued use of our service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

  • Website: https://quantproof.net
  • Response time: Within 30 days for data requests
