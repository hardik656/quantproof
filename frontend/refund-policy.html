How to Request a Refund

  1. Email support@quantproof.net within 30 days of your purchase date

How Refunds Are Processed

Orders paid through Paddle

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all orders placed through Paddle, and Paddle handles returns and refunds for those orders. You can also request a refund directly from Paddle using the link in your Paddle receipt or via paddle.net.

Orders paid through Razorpay (India)

For orders paid in Indian Rupees through Razorpay, QuantProof processes the refund directly.

In all cases, refunds are issued to the original payment method. Once approved, refunds are typically processed within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or card provider.
