⚠ Known limitations — read before uploading ▶
MT5 multi-leg trades — MT5 files with 4-leg or hedged positions may fail parsing. Use the single-strategy validator first to check your MT5 file parses correctly before uploading here.
No date column — correlation is computed positionally (trade 1 vs trade 1), not by date. Meaningless if strategies trade at different frequencies. Always upload dated CSVs.
Minimum 10 trades — each strategy needs at least 10 trades. Strategies below this are rejected.
Correlation is Pearson (linear) — options portfolios with non-linear payoffs need copula-based analysis. Pearson underestimates tail dependence for options strategies.
n=10 per strategy — portfolio runs at n=10 Monte Carlo. Run single-strategy validation at higher n first to verify each strategy individually.
